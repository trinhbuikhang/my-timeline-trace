## doan-van
> A UAV has no line to follow.

It has to hold its own balance, every few milliseconds, against air that never keeps still. Three rotors, not four: one motor short, so the tail has to tilt on a servo just to hold its heading.

One more axis. Every problem got twice as hard.

> And to be honest: it never really flew.

The simulation is what it was supposed to do. In real life it did lift off the ground, but it never held its balance long enough to call it flying. Press REAL LIFE to see roughly how that went. I’m keeping it here because it was my first error I didn’t hide.
