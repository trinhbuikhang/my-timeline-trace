## tieu-de

## doan-van
> A system doesn’t fail because it has error. It fails when someone pretends the error is zero.

In 2022 my error could no longer be hidden. The money was gone. The trust of my friends was gone too, and I lost that myself, because I hadn’t told them the truth. My family was disappointed. My children grew up through months when their father should have been shielding them.

That is how I took off the uniform.

From then on I had to fight on two fronts. One outside: the consequences, the truths I had to say out loud, the people I owed an apology. One inside: myself, the part of me that still wanted to hide, to tell one more lie and be done with it.

> The front inside is the harder one. And it isn’t over.
